What your site is costing you — Starbase Wraps

I'm Jarrett, a web designer in Las Vegas. I looked at your site the way a Tesla owner shopping for tint or PPF would, on their phone.

1. Your "Schedule" buttons don't schedule anything. Both big buttons open an email draft. On a lot of phones that means a blank screen, or an app they never use. Someone ready to book right then has nowhere to pick a day, and no form to fill in. That's the biggest leak on the site.

2. Your pricing page has no way to reach you. Pricing, Services and About have no tappable phone number and no button to book. People who just decided they want the work have to scroll or go back to find you.

3. Google can't see your store items. Each product page looks like a copy of your homepage to Google, so your tint and wrap packages don't show up as their own results.

4. The homepage is slow on phones. In my phone test, the main part of the page took several seconds to appear. The five videos on the homepage are a big part of that.

5. Small cleanups: some buttons are hard to read, the videos and images aren't described for screen readers, and inner page titles are just one word like "Services."

What's working: your pricing is clear and detailed, which most shops don't do, and your phone number is tappable on the homepage.

Unmeasured: visitor numbers, rankings, and how many emails those buttons actually bring in. I didn't contact you or try to buy anything.
