02

Your pricing page has no way to reach you.

Pricing, Services and About have no tappable phone number and no button to book. People who just decided they want the work have to scroll or go back to find you.

03

Each product page looks like a copy of your homepage to Google, so your tint and wrap packages don't show up as their own results.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt doesn't block anything and lists your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    An llms.txt file exists

    starbasewraps.com/llms.txt is live. It lists your main pages, your phone and email, and your location.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML holds about 470 words and a clear main heading ("Tesla Tint, PPF, Color PPF, & vinyl wraps in Las Vegas").

  • Found
    One phone number

    (702) 401-5723 is the only number I found, in the homepage call link, on the services page and in llms.txt.

  • Gap
    No street address

    On the four pages I checked (home, about, services, Tesla pricing) I found no street address. Your location is given as "Southern Highlands/Las Vegas", and llms.txt adds "across I-15 from Southpoint Casino".

  • Gap
    Structured data is thin

    The homepage has local-business markup, but its address field says only "Southern Highlands/Las Vegas". It has no phone number, hours or links to your social profiles, and your web address in it is written without https://.

  • Gap
    No Google listing link

    None of those four pages links to or embeds your Google Maps listing.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 15, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.

Show your full street address, or your service area if you prefer not to publish one, on the site, add it with your phone and hours to the structured data, and link your Google listing from the homepage. That gives search tools clean facts to quote. I can't promise where it will place you.
